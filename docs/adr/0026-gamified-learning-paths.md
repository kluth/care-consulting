# ADR-0026: Gamified Learning Paths & Mastery System

## Status

Proposed

## Date

2026-02-03

## Context

Retention in digital learning is significantly higher when gamification elements are applied. Healthcare workers benefit from clear visual progress and rewards for maintaining their compliance status.

## Decision

Implement **Gamified Learning Paths** with:

1. **Experience Points (XP) & Levels**
   - Earn XP for completing lessons, scenarios, and wellness check-ins.
   - Level up to unlock new avatars and profile badges.

2. **Mastery Progress**
   - Visual "Learning Path" map (Node-based).
   - "Streak" system to encourage daily micro-learning (ADR-0019).

3. **Rewards & Recognition**
   - Facility-wide leaderboards (Optional/Opt-in).
   - Virtual badges for specific achievements (e.g., "Hygiene Hero", "Night Owl Learner").

## Rationale

Gamification triggers intrinsic motivation and makes routine compliance training less tedious. A clear sense of progression reduces drop-off rates.

## Consequences

### Positive
- Increased user engagement.
- Better retention of knowledge.
- Fun factor in professional development.

### Negative
- Risk of over-gamification (must remain professional).
- Effort to maintain and balance the XP system.
