# ADR-0023: Facility Performance Benchmarking (Anonymized)

## Status

Proposed

## Date

2026-02-03

## Context

Facility managers want to know how their team compares to industry standards without violating employee privacy. Benchmarking helps identify areas where extra training or structural changes are needed.

## Decision

Implement **Facility Benchmarking** with:

1. **Aggregation Engine**
   - Calculate facility-wide averages for compliance, burnout risk, and learning speed.
   - Anonymize data at the source.

2. **Benchmark Dashboard**
   - Compare facility data against regional or national averages (anonymized data from all platform users).
   - Only show comparisons if more than 5 facilities are in the peer group (Privacy protection).

3. **Trend Analysis**
   - Track improvement over time compared to the "market" trend.

## Rationale

Drives engagement for managers and helps facilities justify training investments while maintaining strict privacy standards for individual workers.
